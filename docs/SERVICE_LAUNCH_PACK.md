# Service launch pack

CampaignForge AI should be sold first as a small, clear service offer rather than a broad campaign platform.

This launch pack defines the first offer boundary.

## Offer name

Campaign Pack Sprint

## Offer summary

Turn one campaign brief into a reviewed strategy and copy pack with optional concept images and a final export bundle.

## Best fit

- small marketing teams that need first-draft campaign planning quickly
- freelancers who want a repeatable delivery workflow
- agencies that need a lightweight internal accelerator for campaign strategy and copy development

## Included in the first offer

- one campaign brief intake and validation pass
- one generation pass across strategy, angles, copy, CTAs, and prompts
- operator review and cleanup of weak outputs
- one export bundle containing the reviewed deliverables
- optional concept image generation when the chosen delivery mode supports it
- short delivery notes highlighting assumptions, edits, and open points

## Deliverables

- campaign summary
- audience suggestions
- channel recommendations
- campaign angles
- headline variants
- body copy variants
- CTA variants
- image prompts
- optional concept images
- export ZIP bundle
- operator handoff notes

## Explicitly excluded

- media buying or campaign launch execution
- publishing to ad or social platforms
- ongoing optimisation after handoff
- legal sign-off
- enterprise workflow integration
- multi-campaign programme management
- hosted workspace access as part of the initial service

## Turnaround assumption

- initial target: 2 to 3 working days for one campaign pack once the brief is complete

## Revision policy

- one focused revision round is included
- revisions should be limited to the original brief scope
- net-new campaign direction, audience change, or major channel change should be treated as a new sprint

## Operator effort assumption

- designed for one operator or a very small team
- intended to run within a half day to one day of active delivery effort per campaign, excluding client waiting time

## Input requirements

The client should provide:

- product or offer description
- target audience
- goal or campaign objective
- preferred channels
- tone guidance
- banned words or claims
- compliance or legal notes

## Delivery modes

- mock-first mode for safe demos and internal dry runs
- configured provider mode for live delivery when credentials, data handling, and approval boundaries are agreed

## Success criteria

The service is successful if the client leaves with a usable campaign pack that is materially faster to refine and launch than starting from a blank page.

## Positioning rule

If a prospect needs a large collaborative platform, automated publishing, or enterprise workflow tooling, this offer is the wrong fit and should not be stretched to cover that gap.
