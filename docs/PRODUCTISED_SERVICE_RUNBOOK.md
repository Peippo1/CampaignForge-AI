# Productised service runbook

CampaignForge AI can be sold as a done-for-you campaign planning accelerator before it becomes a hosted product.

This workflow is designed for one operator or a small agency team.

## Service promise

Turn a client campaign brief into a reviewed campaign pack containing strategy, audience angles, copy variants, image prompts, concept assets, and an export bundle.

## Intake inputs

- client name and campaign owner
- product or offer description
- target audience
- campaign goal
- primary channels
- tone and brand keywords
- banned words or claims
- compliance or legal notes
- examples of existing campaign material, if available

## Operator workflow

1. Validate the brief
   Check that the goal, audience, channels, and constraints are specific enough to generate useful outputs.
2. Run generation
   Use mock mode for demos or configured provider mode for paid delivery where credentials and data-sharing terms are agreed.
3. Review outputs
   Remove weak angles, tune wording, check claims, and regenerate copy or prompts where needed.
4. Generate image concepts
   Produce concept assets from selected prompts. Treat them as planning assets unless a live image provider and usage rights are explicitly agreed.
5. Approve and export
   Mark useful image concepts, export the campaign bundle, and prepare the client handoff.
6. Deliver notes
   Send a short summary explaining what was generated, what was changed by the operator, and what still needs client approval.

## Deliverables

- campaign summary
- audience or persona suggestions
- channel recommendations
- campaign angles
- headline variants
- body copy variants
- CTA variants
- image prompts
- optional concept images
- export ZIP bundle
- short operator notes

## QA checklist

- brief constraints are reflected in generated copy
- banned words and compliance notes have been checked
- claims are phrased cautiously when evidence is missing
- weak or duplicate variants have been removed from the client-facing pack
- exported bundle opens locally
- generated assets do not include secrets, credentials, or private operational notes

## What not to promise

- ad performance uplift
- automated media buying
- legal approval
- brand-safe final creative without human review
- fully hosted multi-client workspace management

## Follow-on opportunity

Validated service demand should decide whether to invest next in hosted accounts, managed storage, payment flows, or deeper integrations.
